Made in Portugal

A Legacy of Craftsmanship

For years, Iber Cutlery has stood at the intersection of traditional craftsmanship and modern industrial precision. Headquartered in Braga, the historical heart of Portugal's manufacturing region, we have built our reputation on designing, developing, and producing flatware that meets the highest global standards.

We are not just manufacturers; we are engineers, designers, and artisans. From the initial conceptual sketch to the final polished piece, every step of our process is driven by an unyielding dedication to quality. Our state-of-the-art facilities allow us to scale production for large B2B clients without ever losing the meticulous touch of traditional Portuguese cutlery making.

Our Philosophy

The Pillars of Iber Cutlery


Heritage

Deeply rooted in Portugal's rich industrial history, we honor generations of metallurgical expertise. Our heritage informs our work ethic, ensuring that every collection we produce carries a legacy of durability and timeless design.

Innovation

Tradition meets technology. By integrating advanced PVD coating capabilities, in-house mold creation, and highly efficient industrial scaling, we bring modern, resilient, and cutting-edge solutions to the global hospitality and retail markets.

Partnership

We operate as an extension of your brand. We offer unlimited flexibility in custom engraving, bespoke material selection, and packaging, ensuring the final product perfectly aligns with your specific B2B needs and brand identity.

Ready to start your next collection?

Whether you need a fully customized product line developed from scratch or a reliable manufacturing partner to scale your existing designs, our team is ready to help.
