About Us

Iber Cutlery

Iber Cutlery is a distinguished European knife house devoted to precision, heritage, and uncompromising craftsmanship. Founded with a singular vision — to redefine modern cutlery — we blend centuries-old techniques with contemporary engineering to deliver tools that elevate the culinary experience.

Every blade represents a balance between form and function. Designed for professionals yet crafted for connoisseurs, Iber Cutlery stands as a symbol of refined performance and enduring elegance.

Our Heritage

Tradition Refined by Innovation

Rooted in the rich blade-making traditions of the Iberian Peninsula, our philosophy is grounded in meticulous attention to detail. Each knife undergoes a rigorous process of forging, tempering, grinding, and polishing — executed by master artisans with decades of expertise.

By integrating advanced metallurgy with traditional hand-finishing, we ensure superior edge retention, perfect balance, and exceptional durability.

Craftsmanship

Engineered for Excellence

Premium Materials

We source high-carbon stainless steel alloys selected for strength, corrosion resistance, and precise edge geometry.

Hand-Finished Precision

Each blade is individually inspected and hand-polished to guarantee flawless finishing and unmatched sharpness.

Ergonomic Design

Our handles are sculpted for natural grip and balance, ensuring comfort during prolonged professional use.

Global Presence

Trusted Worldwide

Iber Cutlery serves discerning chefs, culinary institutions, and luxury retailers across Europe, North America, and Asia. Our reputation is built upon consistency, reliability, and a commitment to excellence without compromise.

Through strategic partnerships and private label collaborations, we extend our expertise to global markets while maintaining the integrity of our craft.

Our Vision

Precision Without Limits

At Iber Cutlery, we believe that true luxury lies in precision. Our mission is to continue innovating within the world of fine cutlery while preserving the artisanal spirit that defines our identity.

We are committed to sustainable production, responsible sourcing, and continuous refinement — ensuring that every blade we craft today remains exceptional for generations to come.

Iber Cutlery — Excellence Forged in Steel.
