Coating Technology

PVD Technology

PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) technolog is a state-of-the-art surface treatment that allows us to apply a wide range of durable and decorative finishes. PVD coating not only enhances the aesthetic appeal of each piece but also increases scratch resistance, corrosion protection, and overall longevity.

Thanks to PVD, we offer virtually infinite possibilities for combining colors, textures, and finishes. Traditional finishes can be enriched with luxurious PVD tones such as Gold, Champagne, Copper, Anthracite, and Blue. This enables custom-tailored designs that perfectly align with client specifications, from subtle elegance to bold statement pieces.
