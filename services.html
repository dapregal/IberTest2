Services

Custom Cutlery Solutions

At Iber Cutlery, we specialize in delivering fully customized cutlery solutions for global clients. From luxury hotels to high-volume corporate orders, our capacity allows for tailor-made designs, finishes, and packaging adapted to your specific requirements.

Our team collaborates closely with clients to ensure that each piece reflects both functionality and aesthetic excellence. Precision, durability, and elegance are the hallmarks of every order we fulfill.

Capacity

High-Volume Production

Iber Cutlery combines artisanal craftsmanship with industrial-scale production. Our factory is equipped to handle large orders efficiently, without compromising on precision or quality.

Whether your requirement is hundreds or hundreds of thousands of units, our manufacturing processes are optimized for consistent performance, timely delivery, and exceptional finishing.

Customization

Tailored to Your Needs

Engraving & Branding

Laser engraving, embossed logos, and bespoke branding options are available for every product, ensuring your identity is seamlessly integrated.

Material Selection

Choose from premium stainless steel alloys, handles crafted from wood, resin, or composites, all customizable to match your brand aesthetics.

Packaging Solutions

Finishes & Surface Expressions

Each Dart model is available in multiple surface finishes, allowing you to create a distinctive table identity. From polished brilliance to textured matte surfaces and advanced PVD coatings, the possibilities are extensive.

Brilho

High-gloss mirror polish. Elegant and reflective, ideal for formal dining settings.

Areia

Velvet texture with subtle matte softness. Modern and tactile.

Fosco

Minimal matte finish. Clean, architectural, understated luxury.

Pedras

Vintage stone-washed texture. Character and personality in every piece.

PVD Coatings

Gold, Copper, Champagne, Anthracite, Cobalt. Ultra-durable luxury finishes.
